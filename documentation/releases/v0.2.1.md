Adds an offline heating-dashboard generator and bundled JSON/YAML overview and detail templates.

- Include DHW, boost, heating-curve, activity, and energy-source entities.
- Move raw Shelly measurements and diagnostics into a linked Details subview.
- Show current power in W; ignore negative readings for display without treating missing or stale data as zero.
- Show current calendar week/month/year import energy in kWh separately for the heat pump and electric backup.
- Preserve unrelated views during repeated generation and keep concrete entity mappings in local bindings.

This release adds static generation assets without changing heating-control behavior. For generic English public templates and configurable private template overrides, use v0.3.1 or later.
